# Economic Intelligence Platform (EIP)

## Overview

Economic Intelligence Platform (EIP) is an AI-powered platform that collects and analyzes economic and financial news to identify important events, explain why they occurred, assess their impact on different sectors, and provide insights into possible future outcomes. The platform converts complex economic information into understandable intelligence through automated analysis and interactive dashboards.

---

## Problem Statement

Economic and financial information is scattered across multiple sources such as news websites, government reports, and market platforms. Users spend considerable time gathering and interpreting this information before understanding its impact on the economy and financial markets.

The Economic Intelligence Platform addresses this challenge by providing a centralized solution that automatically processes economic news and converts it into meaningful insights.

---

## Objectives

* Collect economic and financial news from multiple trusted sources.
* Detect significant economic events automatically.
* Analyze the causes behind economic developments.
* Evaluate sector-wise and economic impact.
* Generate AI-powered summaries and insights.
* Provide future outlooks based on economic reasoning.
* Offer an interactive dashboard for monitoring economic trends.
* Enable users to interact with an AI-powered economic assistant.

## Key Features

### News Collection

* Fetch economic and financial news from multiple APIs.
* Store and manage news data efficiently.

### News Categorization

Classify news into:

* Inflation
* GDP
* Employment
* Banking
* Monetary Policy
* Commodities
* Forex
* International Markets
* Geopolitical Events

### Sentiment Analysis

Analyze news sentiment as:

* Positive
* Negative
* Neutral

### Event Detection

Identify major economic events such as:

* Interest Rate Changes
* Inflation Reports
* GDP Releases
* Central Bank Announcements
* Commodity Price Shocks

### Impact Analysis

Determine:

* Affected sectors
* Expected market reaction
* Economic consequences

### Economic Health Score

Generate a custom score that reflects the overall condition of the economy using:

* Inflation Data
* GDP Growth
* Employment Data
* Interest Rates
* Market Performance

### Economic Event Analysis

The platform identifies major economic events from daily news and classifies them into categories such as:

* Inflation
* GDP
* Banking
* Employment
* Monetary Policy
* Forex
* Commodities

### Cause Analysis

The system explains why an economic event occurred by analyzing related news and economic indicators.

### Impact Analysis

The system evaluates how different sectors and stakeholders may be affected by the event.

### Future Outlook

The platform provides AI-generated insights about possible future consequences of the event.

### Research Report Generation

Automatically generate:

* Daily Reports
* Weekly Reports
* Monthly Summaries

### AI-Powered Chatbot

Allow users to ask questions such as:

* Why did the market fall today?
* What are the latest inflation updates?
* Summarize today's economic news.
* What happened after the previous interest rate cut?

### Interactive Dashboard

Display:

* News Feed
* Market Sentiment
* Economic Health Score
* Sector Analysis
* Predictions
* Reports
