# Economic Intelligence Platform (EIP)

## Project Name

Economic Intelligence Platform (EIP)

## Domain

Economics, Finance, Artificial Intelligence, Natural Language Processing, Data Analytics

## Project Type

AI-Powered Economic and Financial Intelligence System

## Vision Statement

Build an intelligent platform that transforms daily economic and financial news into structured insights, economic event analysis, impact assessments, and future outlooks through AI-driven analysis.

## Problem Statement

Economic and financial information is distributed across numerous news sources, government publications, and financial platforms.

The lack of a unified system makes it difficult to:

* Track important economic developments
* Understand why economic events occur
* Analyze the impact of events on industries and the economy
* Identify affected sectors
* Understand possible future implications
* Access historical economic insights

## Goal

Create a centralized Economic Intelligence Platform that automatically collects economic news, detects significant events, analyzes their causes, evaluates their impact, and generates actionable economic insights through an interactive dashboard.
