# Economic Intelligence Platform (EIP)

## Project Name

Economic Intelligence Platform (EIP)

## Domain

Economics, Finance, Artificial Intelligence, Natural Language Processing, Data Analytics

## Project Type

AI-Powered Economic and Financial Intelligence System

## Vision Statement

Build an intelligent platform that transforms daily economic and financial news into structured insights, market intelligence, predictions, and research reports through AI-driven analysis.

## Problem Statement

Economic and financial information is distributed across numerous news sources, government publications, and market platforms.

The lack of a unified system makes it difficult to:

* Track important economic developments
* Understand market sentiment
* Measure economic health
* Predict potential market reactions
* Access historical economic insights

## Goal

Create a centralized Economic Intelligence Platform that automates economic analysis and market intelligence generation.
