# Specify

## Functional Requirements

### FR-1 Economic News Collection

* Collect news from financial news sources.
* Fetch articles periodically.
* Store raw news articles.

### FR-2 News Categorization

Categories:

* Inflation
* GDP
* Employment
* Banking
* Monetary Policy
* Commodities
* Forex
* International Markets
* Geopolitical Events

### FR-3 Sentiment Analysis

* Positive
* Negative
* Neutral

### FR-4 Event Detection

Detect:

* RBI Meetings
* Federal Reserve Announcements
* Inflation Reports
* GDP Releases
* Interest Rate Changes

### FR-5 Impact Analysis

* Sector Influence
* Market Impact
* Economic Consequences

### FR-6 Economic Health Score

Calculate score using:

* Inflation
* GDP
* Employment
* Interest Rates
* Market Performance

### FR-7 Market Prediction Engine

Predict:

* Bullish
* Bearish
* Neutral

### FR-8 Research Report Generation

Generate:

* Daily Reports
* Weekly Reports
* Monthly Reports

### FR-9 RAG Chatbot

Answer:

* Economic Questions
* Market Questions
* Historical Queries

### FR-10 Dashboard

Display:

* News Feed
* Sentiment
* Economic Health Score
* Sector Analysis
* Predictions
* Reports

## Non-Functional Requirements

* High Performance
* Scalability
* Security
* Reliability
* Maintainability
